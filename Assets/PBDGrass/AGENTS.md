# PBD grass workspace

- This directory is the active workspace for PBD grass generation, simulation and rendering.
- Keep future PBD grass changes and new dependencies inside `Assets/PBDGrass/`.
- Treat the pre-existing code and resources elsewhere in Assets as archive references. Do not edit, move, rename, or replace them unless the user explicitly requests it.
- Keep the copied assembly, namespaces, shaders and Unity asset GUIDs independent of archived assets.
- Do not access `Library/`; the root project exclusion instructions also apply here.
- This extraction preserves the original algorithms. Make upgrades as explicit changes to this copy, and document meaningful behavior changes.
- Performance target: RTX 3080 Ti, 32,768 active XPBD blades, at most 1 ms of GPU solving plus geometry reconstruction per complete simulation tick. Preserve the accepted long-leaf, elastic rebound and plastic lodging behavior when optimizing. Report culling, rendering and CPU management separately; do not claim success by averaging in frames without simulation or reducing the active blade count. Use GPU timestamps rather than synchronized CPU/readback timings.
- The optimization acceptance baseline is `Scenes/PBDGrassCombinedBaseline.unity`: 32,768 active XPBD blades surrounded by 98,304 baked Bezier blades, with moving near/middle LOD transitions. The old XPBD-only benchmark is a diagnostic, not sufficient acceptance evidence. Preserve existing response-bank compatibility and verify transient rebakes of all eight materials against the accepted bank; never overwrite accepted bake assets just to make an optimization pass. Run the combined workload, handoff/plastic regression and eight-type wind-response comparison when validating solver changes. Record any required rebake or data-format migration explicitly before accepting such a change.
- The tiered-state experiment is `Scenes/PBDGrassTieredState.unity`, enabled explicitly by `GrassHandoffSettings.UseTieredState` and its separate compute shader. Preserve the original baseline scene and default path. Keep full display history in bounded boundary/transition slots and share final render geometry with XPBD; do not restore global full-node history or stable-near duplicate reconstruction to mask a handoff bug. Report reserved history capacity separately from resident slots, GPU buffer byte counts separately from total VRAM, and CPU submission cost alongside GPU timing. Cold entries outside the warm band use estimated motion; do not claim exact historical velocity recovery for that case.
